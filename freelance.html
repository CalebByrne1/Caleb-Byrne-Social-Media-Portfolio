04 — About

Builder.
Creator.
Obsessive.

I'm a creator who loves making things. Whether that's producing a beat in FL Studio, editing a video in DaVinci Resolve, or building a piece of software — creating is where I spend most of my time.

My content spans tutorials, product demos, and talking head videos about topics I'm genuinely into. I have 465K+ all-time views across two YouTube channels, where I've published 56 full-length videos alongside Shorts, and 100K+ TikTok views in the past year across two accounts.

I have an Associate's degree in Software Development, and I'm pursuing a Bachelor of Science (B.S.) in Digital Content Creation at Dakota State University — which means I think about content technically as well as creatively. I understand hooks, retention curves, and platform analytics, not just aesthetics.

Outside of work and school: I make my own social media content, make beats, read books, play video games (anybody else ready for GTA VI?), and occasionally do photography.
