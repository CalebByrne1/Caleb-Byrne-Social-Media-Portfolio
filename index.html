The Goal

Answer the questions players still type into YouTube about an older game, where demand is steady and competition is thin.

The Choices

Each guide answers one question in under 90 seconds. Titles use the player's own words, like "How To Get Caps In Fallout New Vegas," so they match real searches. Once the caps videos landed, I built a series around them: Lucky, reaching the Strip at level 1, and money glitches.

What I Learned

One search win is worth building on. Guides from 2023 still earned 316 of the channel's 421 views from Sep 25 to Oct 22, 2026. Evergreen search content keeps working long after launch week.

Taking the Caps Guide to TikTok

The same Fallout: New Vegas caps guide reached 161K views on TikTok, nearly 14× its YouTube views, and logged a third as much watch time as the entire YouTube channel.

161KViews on one TikTok
803Hours watched
2,576Saves
91.2%Views from TikTok search
Read the breakdownClose

The Goal

Get more out of a guide that was already working on YouTube by putting it in front of a second audience.

The Choices

I record once and post the same video to both platforms, because their search behaves similarly. What changes is the packaging: a title, description, and hashtags written for each platform's audience. It paid off: the top TikTok queries were "fallout new vegas" and "fallout new vegas tips." When a commenter asked for more, I replied with a stealth boy follow-up, which reached 17K views.

What I Learned

Where a video wins depends on how people find it. The 2023 search guides grew far larger on TikTok, while the 2025 Unlimited Caps guide did better on YouTube. The 2,576 saves show viewers bookmarking it as a reference.

The BEST Open-Back Headphones Under $100!

A single 6:06 review that produced 23% of the channel's lifetime watch time and is still its top video 18 months later.

82.8%Views from YouTube search
6,514Views, 138 hours watched

Read the breakdownClose

The Goal

Answer a buying question people type into YouTube: which budget open-back headphones are worth it.

The Choices

The title and "BUDGET KING" thumbnail match buying-intent searches like "best open back headphones" and "best headphones under 100." That match is why click-through runs 3.1 points above the channel average.

What I Learned

It's still growing, with 862 views from Sep 25 to Oct 22, 2026, about 2.5× its lifetime pace. But viewers watch 25% on average, and the end screen gets no clicks. Next time: chapters for each headphone, a faster first verdict, and an end screen pointing to a related video.
