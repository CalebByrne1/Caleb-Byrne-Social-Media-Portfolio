About

Builder.
Creator.
Obsessive.

I'm a creator who loves making things. Whether that's producing a beat in FL Studio, cutting a video in DaVinci Resolve, or building a piece of software — creating is where I spend most of my time.

My content spans gaming, how-to tutorials for video editing and music production, and topics I'm genuinely into. I run two YouTube channels and two TikTok accounts, with over 400,000 combined YouTube views and 100,000+ TikTok views in the past year.

I'm studying software development and digital content creation at Dakota State University — which means I think about content technically as well as creatively. I understand hooks, retention curves, and platform analytics, not just aesthetics.

Outside of work and school: I make beats, read books, play video games (anybody else ready for GTA VI?), occasionally do photography, and of course make my own social media content.
